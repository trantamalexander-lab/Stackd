FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Stackd free?

You can download and start finding flips for free. Pro unlocks more scans and priority results — you subscribe on the web and it unlocks across the app.

Are the deals real?

Yes. Stackd only shows flips backed by real, recent sold prices — and it filters out fakes, damaged listings, and prices that are too good to be true.

Do I need experience to flip?

No. Stackd gives you the buy price, sell price, profit, and a step-by-step guide for each flip. Start with what you already know.

Which platforms does it cover?

eBay, StockX, GOAT, Depop, Poshmark and more — for sneakers, streetwear, cards, and games.
